# DES Series Standard — Self-Inquiry Protocol

**Date:** 7. Mai 2026
**Status:** DRAFT — subject to revision

## Purpose

Standardized protocol for Desi self-inquiry runs across Papers 8+.
Applied after each paper's main experiment, before the next Design Memo.

## Configuration

- Operator lens: `recursive_modulation` (Mozart)
- Max loops: 5
- Anti-Delphi: enabled
- Models: DS4_GPT4o
- No early_saturation intervention

## Output requirements

- Raw ClaimGraph preserved (no curation)
- Negative findings section mandatory
- Yield classification: high / medium / low / null
- Label: EXPLORATORY — not pre-registered, not confirmatory

## Usage constraint

Self-inquiry findings must NOT be used to modify:
- The paper's main text retroactively
- The next paper's seed (use Desi Consultation for that)
- Pre-registration documents after the fact
